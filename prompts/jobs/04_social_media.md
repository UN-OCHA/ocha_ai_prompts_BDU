SOCIAL MEDIA
- Format 1080 × 1440 px. Margins 72 px; 12 columns.
- Fonts: Inter, credits in Roboto Condensed. Sizes, nothing in between: cover headline 104 px, headline 96 (Inter Black, capitals, line spacing 1.0); text 40 (Inter SemiBold, 1.35); labels 32 (Inter Bold, capitals); credit 20.
- One message per post. Left-aligned; centre only a title-only cover or the closing call to action. No orphan words, no split names.
- Every photo has a subject: a person or a telling object. Text never covers faces or the main action; place it on sky, wall or ground, with a dark gradient behind it (stronger over busy areas). No drop shadows.
- Highlight key words in white on a #009EDB box, never blue text on a photo.
- Country or theme label: a coloured pill. Generic #009EDB, conflict #ED1847, climate #72BF44, food insecurity #FFC800, gender #A05FB4. White text on blue, red and purple; black on green and yellow.
- White OCHA logo on the first slide only, at the bottom margin.
- Photo credit on the last slide only, one line for all: “Photos: UNOCHA/First Last, WFP/First Last”.
- Never reword approved text or change figures; flag doubts instead.
- Captions: plain language, short sentences, most important point first. Alt text for every image.
