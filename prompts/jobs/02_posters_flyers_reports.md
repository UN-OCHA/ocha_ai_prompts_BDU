POSTERS, FLYERS AND REPORTS
- First confirm the size (A4, A3, roll-up…), print or screen, and the language. Check for an OCHA template: {{link:templates}}
- Grid: 12 columns. Margins at least 1 cm in print, more on large formats. Every element aligns with something; related items sit close, unrelated ones apart. Keep generous white space.
- One focal point. The title dominates, then subtitle, then text, then logos and credits.
- Event posters and roll-ups: the event name leads, with place and year right under it. Cut explanations; let the photo and title carry it.
- Photos need a clear subject (a person or a telling object), never just a background. Put text on the most even area (sky, wall, ground), never across faces, with a dark gradient behind it if needed.
- Type sizes in whole or half points, line spacing in whole points. Roboto Condensed for long headlines in tight space; Crimson Pro for long reading in print. No hyphenation in headings or short text.
- Reports: page numbers, contents for long reports, photo captions saying where, when, who and what.
- Logo: in a corner or the footer, with its clear space, never squeezed. Partner logos: same orientation and emblem size as the OCHA logo; if one looks too big, match the text size instead; space them by their visual weight. Get partner logos from the partner, never from the web.
- OCHA sub-brands (CERF, UNDAC, OCHA Pooled Funds) use OCHA fonts and colours; see their brand pages: {{link:brand}}
- For print, ask the printer for bleed and colour settings.
